The Retreat

Theeram means the shore — and that is exactly where you are.

Set on a quiet stretch of the Kumbalam backwaters, just minutes from Kochi, Theeram Backwaters is a private eighty-cent retreat where whitewashed walls, terracotta roofs and old shade trees give way to a lawn that runs all the way to the water's edge.

It was built for the kind of day a family remembers — a wedding under an open sky, a milestone dinner as the light fades over the water, a celebration that feels both rooted and effortless. Traditional Kerala in its bones, gently modern in every comfort.

01 · Exclusive Use

The Full Property

The whole shoreline, yours for the day.

For weddings and grand celebrations, take all of Theeram. The grounds, the pandal, the air-conditioned Anchor Villa and the suites come together as one private property for 200–250 guests — room for a ceremony and a reception, a green room for the family, and a place to stay the night, all behind a single gate.

200–250 guests Private exclusive use Grounds + villa + suites
02 · The Signature Space

Pandal & Lawns

An open pavilion, the water on every side.

The heart of Theeram is its pandal — a soaring thatched pavilion that opens completely to the backwaters, framed by coconut palms and a lawn that catches the evening light. It seats around two hundred for a ceremony, reception or seated dinner, with the gardens flowing around it for cocktails, a sadhya, or a mandap by the shore.

~200 seated guests Open-air thatched pavilion Waterfront lawn to the shore
03 · Anchor Villa

Anchor Villa

A cool, calm heart to the retreat.

Fully air-conditioned and dressed in cane, teak and soft sage-green joinery, the Anchor Villa is where smaller gatherings settle in — an intimate dinner, a family lunch, a corporate session of 25 to 30. Open-plan living and dining, a quiet bar corner, and tall green-framed windows looking onto the gardens.

25–30 guests Air-conditioned throughout Heritage interiors
04 · Ivory Suite

Ivory Suite

Heritage, in miniature.

An air-conditioned private room wrapped in patterned tile, beamed ceilings and old-world calm — ideal for an intimate gathering of around ten, or a serene retreat off the main lawn.

~10 guests
